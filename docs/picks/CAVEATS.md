# ADW picks — things to check before publishing

## From the Broadsheet / CityMag beat

- **Part Time Lover** and **Restaurant Botanic** have no street number anywhere
  (Pilgrim Lane; the Botanic Garden rotunda). Not invented — they need a
  hand-placed pin like the venues on the program map.
- **Press\* Food & Wine** — 40 Waymouth St came from the agent's general
  knowledge, not a fetched page. Verify.
- **Leigh Street Wine Room** — the Studio-Gram credit is from Studio-Gram's own
  project index, not press. Year 2019 is an estimate.
- **Africola** — credit solid, year approximate (opened late 2014).
- **Clever Little Tailor** is by **Adam Johnson Design**, not Sans-Arc. Search
  results commonly misattribute this one.
- **Shadow Baking** has two sites; only the Glenelg one is well confirmed.

## Deliberately excluded, easy to add back

- **Fishbank** (2 King William St) — credit is shared across five people
  (Claire Kneebone with Folland Panozzo, Mark Folland, Mo Hardy, Ali Palmer).
- **Madre** (57 Gilbert St, Chris Rowlands, 2019) — Broadsheet lists it as

- Verified but cut for length: Sunny's Pizza (17 Solomon St, Studio-Gram),
  Abbots & Kinney (78 Pirie St, Studio-Gram), Hnly (Henley Beach, Genesin),

## Gap

Pubs are thin — only the Strathmore could be credited. Adelaide's other
renovated pubs largely aren't credited in the press. Worth a dedicated pass.

- Dulux Colour Awards and INDE.Awards returned no SA hospitality — not proof
  there are none, only that they did not surface.
- EDDA 2012-2014, 2017 and 2021 are thinly covered. The per-category galleries

## Classics beat — judgement calls

- **Paul's Seafood on Gouger** (1946, on Gouger since 1957) left OUT of the
  saved list: Quandoo and OpenTable still list it, RestaurantGuru marks it
  permanently closed. Worth a phone call — it would be a good entry.
- **T-Chow** left out: a genuine institution, but substantially refitted in
  2023 with new VIP rooms and a rear bar, so it fails the "largely unchanged
  room" test. Easy to add if you read the criterion more loosely.
- **Concubine** kept, but flagged by the researcher as the least unchanged of
  the Gouger Street group — a styled fitout rather than an enduring room.
- Cut with reasons: Botanic Hotel (gutted, reopened smaller 1998),
  Crown & Sceptre (heritage-listed but redeveloped), Mercato (institution
  since 1972 but now in a brand-new 2025 build, so no enduring room).
- Two more State Heritage pubs available if the list wants lengthening:
  Prince Albert, 254-256 Wright St (1850s, largely unaltered) and
  The Metropolitan, 42-46 Grote St (1883).

**No surviving heritage neon** on an Adelaide food venue — the researcher
looked. Beehive Corner is heritage-listed but its Claude Neon sign is gone
and it is not a food venue.

Years to treat as approximate: Gaucho's (c.1985, inferred from a review),
Zuma (c.1994, undated trader page), Marino (market says 1976, other sources
say the family bought an older shop in 1975), Wheatsheaf (1878 is the safe
figure, some sources push it earlier), Bakery on O'Connell (unpublished).
